WHAT'S INCLUDED

Free hosting, no catch — here's exactly what that means

We'd rather tell you upfront what's covered and where it stops than have you find out later.

INCLUDED

  • Unlimited trafficA busy day, a viral post, a full house — your bill doesn't change.
  • Free SSL / HTTPSThe padlock, and the trust it signals, on by default.
  • Fast everywhereYour site loads quickly whether a visitor's in Bend or across the country.
  • Use your own domainBring your own domain and we set it up for you.
  • Zero server maintenanceNothing to patch, renew, or lock you out if a password gets forgotten.
  • Built-in SEO basicsEvery page ships with meta tags, sitemap, and schema markup — the groundwork search engines look for.

WHERE IT STOPS

  • No built-in databaseThe site can't run member logins or a shopping cart on its own — those get added on top.
  • No site-wide searchSearching a large site (a big menu, a resource library) isn't automatic — it's an add-on.
  • No e-commerce or booking backendOnline ordering or appointment booking need a payment or booking integration layered in.
  • No email hostingThis hosts your website, not your inbox. A professional @yourbusiness.com address is a separate add-on.
  • Forms need a form handlerContact forms route through a free third-party service, not a server we run — reliable, but worth knowing.

Most small business sites never hit these limits. If yours will, that's exactly what the add-ons below are for.
